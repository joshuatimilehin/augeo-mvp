# Augeo Terms & Refund Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

## What you're buying

A credit pack gives you a set number of flyer credits. Each flyer you generate uses 1 credit. Credits don't expire, but reminder messages will pause if you opt out (reply STOP).

## What a flyer credit gets you

- One generated flyer, in the style and language you choose, based on the product photo and details you provide.
- A free low-resolution preview with a watermark before you spend a credit — you only pay when you're happy enough to unlock the full version.
- Reasonable regeneration if something clearly went wrong on our end (garbled text, badly cropped photo, wrong price shown) — message us and we'll fix it, usually without charging another credit.

## What it doesn't guarantee

- We can't guarantee how a flyer performs once you post it — that depends on your product, pricing, and audience, not just the design.
- Minor style variation between flyers is intentional (we deliberately vary layouts so your posts don't look repetitive) — this isn't a defect.

## Refunds

- **Unused credits**: refundable within 7 days of purchase if you haven't used any credits from that pack yet. Message us with your payment reference.
- **Used credits**: not refundable, since the flyer has already been generated and delivered — but see "what a flyer credit gets you" above for our fix policy on our-side errors.
- **Failed payments**: if Paystack charged you but you didn't receive credits, message us with your payment reference and we'll resolve it — either crediting your account or refunding the charge.
- Refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Paystack, typically within 5-10 business days.

## Your content

- You confirm you have the right to use any product photo you send us.
- You own the flyers we generate for you and can use them however you like.
- We design each flyer from scratch based on your product and our own style templates — we don't copy from other sellers' ads, and we run automated checks to keep our own output from repeating itself too closely.

## Fair use

Augeo is built for real product marketing. We reserve the right to pause accounts that use the service for spam, harassment, or clearly unrelated/abusive content.

## Changes

We may update these terms as the service grows. Material changes will be announced via WhatsApp to active customers.

## Contact

Questions, disputes, or refund requests — message us on WhatsApp and ask for "support."
