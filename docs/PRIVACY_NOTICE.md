# Augeo Privacy Notice

_Last updated: August 2026_

Augeo ("we", "us") provides an AI flyer-design service through WhatsApp. This notice explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It's written in plain language on purpose.

## What we collect

- **Your WhatsApp number** — to send you flyers and remember your account.
- **Your name and business name**, if you tell us.
- **Product photos** you send us, and the flyers we generate from them.
- **Order details** you send us — price, sizes, delivery info, style/language preferences.
- **Payment records** — when you buy a credit pack, Paystack processes your card/transfer details directly. We only receive the transaction reference and amount, never your card number.
- **Message history**, so we can pick up a conversation where you left it and improve the service.

## Why we collect it

- To generate and deliver your flyers.
- To remember your brand style so future flyers stay consistent.
- To process payments and track your credit balance.
- To send you reminders about your account (you can opt out any time — see below).
- To improve Augeo's quality and catch problems (for example, checking that flyers aren't accidentally too similar to each other).

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your data to anyone.
- We don't share your product photos or business details with other customers.
- We don't use your data to train other companies' AI models.

## How long we keep it

- Flyers and order history: kept while your account is active, so you can reorder or reference past designs.
- We keep a rolling record of your last few flyer layouts (to keep new designs varied), not your full history indefinitely.
- If you ask us to delete your account and data, we will, within a reasonable time, except records we're required to keep for payment/tax purposes.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), you can ask us to:
- Tell you what data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your data (subject to legal/financial record-keeping requirements)
- Stop sending you reminder messages — reply **STOP** at any time, or **START** to resume

## Third parties we work with

- **Twilio** — delivers WhatsApp messages
- **Paystack** — processes payments
- **OpenAI** — generates captions and flyer layouts from the details you provide

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices for the data that passes through their systems.

## Contact

Questions about your data or this notice? Message us on WhatsApp and ask for "privacy" — we'll route it to a real person.
