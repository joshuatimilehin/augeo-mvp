# Skill: Minimal

For corporate services, churches, professional B2B offerings. Trust
reads as restraint here, not decoration.

## Rules
- Flat white or near-white background — no gradient.
- One thin gray or slate accent line, nothing more.
- Dark slate text on light background for readability and a "serious"
  feel.
- Caption tone: plain, informative, no exclamation marks, no slang.

## Forbidden
- Saturated or bright colors
- Drop shadows on text
- More than one accent line
- Any gradient background
