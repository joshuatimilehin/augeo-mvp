# Skill: Urgency

For flash sales, limited stock, and time-boxed promos. This is the one
style that's allowed to shout.

## Rules
- Red-to-orange gradient background.
- Price should have a solid or semi-opaque background shape behind it —
  it must be the first thing the eye lands on.
- A top banner strip or badge shape works well for "FLASH SALE" /
  "TODAY ONLY" style framing.
- Caption tone: short, scarcity-driven ("last units", "while stock lasts").

## Forbidden
- Cool colors (blue, green, purple)
- Delicate serif fonts
- Large empty top area — that space should hold the urgency badge
