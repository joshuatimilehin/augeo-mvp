# Skill: Festive

For holidays and celebrations — Christmas, Eid, Valentine's, "detty
December", anniversaries. Should stay generic enough to work across all
of them; don't hard-code Christmas-specific imagery unless the product
description says so.

## Rules
- Warm green-to-emerald gradient as the default base, gold accents.
- A decorative frame border or corner ornaments read as "celebration"
  without needing literal holiday icons.
- Caption tone: warm, a little festive, still clear on price and CTA.

## Forbidden
- Cold gray or steel-blue tones
- Corporate/minimal styling (that's the minimal skill's job)
- A flat single color with zero decoration — festive needs *some* ornament
