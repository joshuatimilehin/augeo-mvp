# Skill: Luxury

For high-ticket fashion, beauty, and premium goods. The customer is paying
a premium price and the flyer should not undercut that with clutter.

## Rules
- Dark background (near-black or deep navy), gold or cream accents only.
- Never more than one accent line and one decorative shape.
- Price is confident, not urgent — no countdowns, no "today only".
- Photo gets a soft shadow and, where the layout allows, rounded corners.
- Generous negative space. If in doubt, remove an element rather than add one.
- Caption tone: understated, aspirational, short.

## Forbidden
- Red, orange, or neon colors
- Clip-art style icons or emoji-heavy captions
- More than 3 total decorative accents
- Comic or playful fonts
