# Skill: Bold

For streetwear, fast fashion, food, and anything aimed at a young,
trend-driven buyer. Energy over elegance.

## Rules
- Warm, saturated gradient background (orange/amber family).
- Diagonal or angular decorative shapes are welcome — this is the one
  style where sharp edges beat soft ones.
- Price and caption can be large and close together, near-competing for
  attention.
- Caption tone: punchy, short sentences, exclamation marks allowed.

## Forbidden
- Pastel or muted colors
- Thin, delicate accent lines
- Large empty negative space (that's the luxury skill's job)
- Serif or script fonts
